# શ્રી કલોલ કચ્છ કડવા પાટીદાર સમાજ
## Shree Kalol Kutch Kadva Patidar Samaj (SKKKPS)

Official digital portal for Shree Kalol Kutch Kadva Patidar Samaj, Kalol, Gandhinagar (Gujarat).

### 🏛️ Modules Included:
1. **સમાજ પરિચય (Samaj Details)**: Community heritage, history, core values, Kuldevi Maa Umiya blessings, and Bhavan information in Kalol.
2. **સમાજના હોદ્દેદારો (Persons of Samaj)**: Managing committee, office bearers, youth & women wings, native Kutch villages, and contact directory.
3. **ઇવેન્ટ ફોટો (Event Photos)**: Cultural celebrations, Sharad Purnima Raas-Garba, and youth merit felicitation gallery with lightbox zoom.
4. **ઇવેન્ટ કેલેન્ડર (Event Calendar)**: Upcoming festival dates with traditional Gujarati Tithi, timings, venue, and attendance RSVP.
